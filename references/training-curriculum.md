# FDE Training Curriculum

Use this curriculum selectively. It is a menu of learning modules, not a fixed course that every candidate must complete in full.

## Learning design

For each selected competency:
1. State the capability and why it matters.
2. Teach the minimum useful concepts.
3. Demonstrate with a realistic example.
4. Give a guided exercise.
5. Give an independent exercise in a different context.
6. Assess the artifact and reasoning.
7. Record evidence and decide whether to advance, repeat or adapt.

A candidate has not mastered a topic merely by repeating a definition.

## Modules

### M1 — Business Acumen
**Topics:** Business models, value chains, revenue/cost drivers, unit economics, profitability, operational KPIs, risk, investment cases.

**Practice:** Analyze an unfamiliar business; identify value drivers and formulate measurable business questions.

**Evidence:** Business overview, KPI tree, value-leakage hypotheses, basic cost-benefit estimate.

### M2 — Customer and Business Discovery
**Topics:** Stakeholder mapping, interviewing, observation, contextual inquiry, active listening, unknowns, problem framing.

**Practice:** Conduct a simulated discovery interview and distinguish stakeholder claims from observed facts.

**Evidence:** Stakeholder map, interview guide, discovery notes, validated problem statement.

### M3 — Process Engineering
**Topics:** As-is/to-be mapping, swimlanes, handoffs, queues, bottlenecks, exceptions, SOPs, process variation, value-stream thinking.

**Practice:** Map a process from observation and identify failure points.

**Evidence:** Process map, exception map, improvement hypotheses.

### M4 — Critical Thinking and Root-Cause Analysis
**Topics:** Hypothesis-driven investigation, 5 Whys, fishbone, fault trees, causal reasoning, sampling, falsification, competing explanations.

**Practice:** Investigate a discrepancy with conflicting stakeholder accounts and incomplete evidence.

**Evidence:** Hypothesis/evidence matrix, root-cause argument, unresolved unknowns.

### M5 — Data and Evidence
**Topics:** Data quality, SQL/Excel analysis, transaction tracing, reconciliation, sampling, distributions, baselines, metric design, data lineage.

**Practice:** Analyze a realistic operational dataset and trace an exception end-to-end.

**Evidence:** Reproducible analysis, data-quality findings, baseline KPIs.

### M6 — Solution Engineering
**Topics:** Requirements derived from discovery, architecture options, integration patterns, APIs, workflow automation, build-vs-buy, security, reliability, maintainability.

**Practice:** Design multiple options for a validated problem and justify the smallest effective intervention.

**Evidence:** Solution options, architecture sketch, trade-off matrix, risk register.

### M7 — Technical Execution and Delivery
**Topics:** Rapid prototyping, testing, observability, deployment, CI/CD, integration, rollback, production support, iterative delivery.

**Practice:** Build and demonstrate a small working prototype or integration.

**Evidence:** Working demo, tests, deployment notes, operational handover.

### M8 — AI and Technology Judgment
**Topics:** AI vs rules/software, feasibility, data readiness, evaluation, hallucination/error risk, human-in-the-loop, privacy, security, cost and monitoring.

**Practice:** Assess an AI request and recommend AI, conventional automation, process change or no build.

**Evidence:** Feasibility memo, evaluation plan, risk and cost analysis.

### M9 — Stakeholder Management and Change
**Topics:** Communication, conflict, expectation management, resistance, facilitation, training, adoption, incentives and organizational dynamics.

**Practice:** Role-play a stakeholder conflict and negotiate a safe pilot.

**Evidence:** Stakeholder plan, decision log, adoption plan.

### M10 — Ownership and Business Impact
**Topics:** Outcome ownership, baseline/target, ROI, adoption metrics, operational accountability, feedback loops, iteration.

**Practice:** Create an outcome plan for a proposed intervention and respond to a pilot that misses its target.

**Evidence:** KPI tree, measurement plan, ownership matrix, iteration decision.

## Suggested sequencing

1. Business Acumen + Discovery.
2. Process Engineering + Critical Thinking.
3. Data & Evidence.
4. Solution Engineering + AI Judgment.
5. Technical Execution.
6. Stakeholder Management + Ownership.
7. Integrated field simulation.

Change the sequence when the candidate's evidence shows a different prerequisite or learning need.

## Training quality

- Prefer realistic work samples over lectures.
- Use a different business context for transfer tests.
- Teach candidates to state uncertainty and design a way to resolve it.
- Include non-software interventions.
- Do not equate AI use with innovation.
- Include privacy, security, safety and operational risk when relevant.
