# FDE Competency Framework

## Definition

A Forward Deployed Engineer works close to real users and business operations to discover important problems, translate them into engineering interventions, deploy those interventions in context, and own measurable outcomes. The work combines software engineering with business discovery, systems thinking, communication and execution.

FDE work is not limited to process engineering or AI implementation. Some opportunities require software; others require process redesign, integration, data improvements, organizational changes, or no new technology.

## Competencies and weights

| ID | Competency | Weight | Evidence to seek |
|---|---|---:|---|
| C1 | Business Acumen | 10% | Understands business model, value drivers, costs, risks, KPIs and investment constraints |
| C2 | Problem Discovery | 15% | Finds problems without requirements; observes, interviews, identifies unknowns and validates pain |
| C3 | Process Engineering | 10% | Maps workflows, handoffs, exceptions, dependencies and bottlenecks; distinguishes actual from prescribed work |
| C4 | Critical Thinking | 15% | Tests hypotheses, challenges assumptions, considers alternatives and updates conclusions with evidence |
| C5 | Data & Evidence Analysis | 10% | Checks data quality, traces transactions, quantifies impact, establishes baselines and validates findings |
| C6 | Solution Engineering | 10% | Chooses proportionate interventions; considers architecture, integration, build/buy, security and operational fit |
| C7 | Technical Execution | 10% | Prototypes, tests, integrates, deploys, debugs and supports reliable operation |
| C8 | AI & Technology Judgment | 5% | Chooses AI only when justified; understands feasibility, limitations, cost, privacy and alternatives |
| C9 | Stakeholder Management | 5% | Listens, communicates, handles conflict/resistance and builds trust across roles |
| C10 | Ownership & Business Impact | 10% | Drives adoption, accountability, outcome measurement, iteration and business results |

## Rating anchors

| Level | Label | Behavioral anchor |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Not demonstrated | Cannot identify relevant issues or give a coherent approach |
| 2 | Emerging | Recognizes issues but reasoning is generic, incomplete or needs substantial direction |
| 3 | Developing | Gives a workable approach but misses meaningful validation, dependencies, trade-offs or execution details |
| 4 | Independent | Investigates independently, uses evidence, makes defensible decisions and accounts for business consequences |
| 5 | Advanced | Handles complex ambiguity, tests competing explanations, anticipates downstream consequences and can guide others |

## Evidence types

- Demonstrated: observed directly in the interview, work sample or practical exercise.
- Claimed: candidate describes prior experience without direct validation.
- Not assessed: no adequate opportunity to observe.

Record evidence and confidence separately. A candidate may score well on verbal reasoning but have no demonstrated hands-on delivery evidence. State this explicitly.

## Weighted score

For scored competencies:
`weighted contribution = (rating / 5) × competency weight`

Sum contributions to obtain a percentage. If a competency is not assessed, do not silently treat it as zero or five. Report the score as provisional and show coverage. For a complete score, all ten dimensions need sufficient evidence.

## Critical readiness gates

Do not recommend independent FDE readiness unless each of the following is at least level 3 with demonstrated evidence:
- C2 Problem Discovery
- C4 Critical Thinking
- C5 Data & Evidence Analysis
- C6 Solution Engineering
- C7 Technical Execution
- C10 Ownership & Business Impact

A high weighted score cannot compensate for a critical gate failure.

## Development classifications

Use these as guidance, not automatic hiring decisions:

- **FDE Ready – Advanced:** Strong independent evidence across all critical gates; can lead complex engagements.
- **FDE Ready:** Demonstrated ability to handle standard engagements independently.
- **FDE Associate:** Can contribute effectively under an experienced FDE.
- **FDE Potential:** Promising capability but needs targeted development and supervised practice.
- **Not Yet Demonstrated:** Evidence currently insufficient; further development or assessment required.

Do not use "not suitable" as a permanent label. Reassess after development and in different contexts.
